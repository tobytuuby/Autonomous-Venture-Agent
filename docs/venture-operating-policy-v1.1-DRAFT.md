**Runtime status:** The current repository flow is synthetic. This document does not enable live authorities, provider calls, capital movement, customer/vendor contact, or venture execution.

## Confirmed operating rules

### Seed capital and venture selection

- The initial project seed budget is **$1,000**, supporting up to **ten initial ventures** at no more than **$100 of seed capital per venture**.
- A venture may not draw from another venture's seed allocation or receive more than its own $100 initial seed from the CEO pool.
- Before funding, the CEO must assess whether a proposed venture is likely to be profitable. Every new venture must first be discussed with the owner and receive explicit durable approval for its exact proposal/version and scope before CEO greenlight, funding, launch or manager execution. The CEO cannot approve on the owner's behalf.
- The CEO should provide its evidence, assumptions, expected costs and revenue, and rationale with its decision. A profitability estimate is not a guarantee.
- The CEO may leave seed capital unallocated when no proposal meets its viability assessment. A funding ceiling is not an instruction to spend.
- After owner approval, a manager may autonomously delegate within the approved venture bounds. Routine contact within those bounds does not require a separate conversation per contact, but independently authorized live/contact permissions remain necessary and currently denied.

### Venture earnings

- Track receipts, operating costs, profit, and retained funds separately for each venture.
- For this operating process, taxes are handled by the user outside the agent workflow. The agents do not estimate, reserve, or pay taxes.
- The venture's profit is divided **80% usable by that same venture** and **20% saved**. The 80% may fund that venture's growth; it does not increase its initial CEO-funded seed cap or become a shared CEO spending balance.
- When a venture reaches **$100 in cumulative profit**, its accumulated saved 20% is returned to the CEO pool.

### CEO pool after the initial seed

- The CEO pool may use no more than a **50% share** to seed additional ventures as the pool grows beyond the initial $1,000 seed budget.
- That 50% is a ceiling, not an automatic launch trigger. The CEO must still assess a proposal as viable, obtain owner discussion and explicit proposal-specific approval, and may leave funds unspent.
- Any additional venture remains subject to the **$100 maximum seed allocation**.

## Open policy details

These points were not settled by the user in the conversation and must not be silently treated as approved settings:

1. **50/50 calculation base:** whether the 50% maximum applies to each new amount entering the CEO pool or to the pool's cumulative available balance.
2. **Profit split after the $100 milestone:** the rule says to return the accumulated saved 20% when cumulative profit reaches $100; routing of later 20% shares has not been confirmed.
3. **Stop condition for an unprofitable venture:** the $100 seed cap is established, but a time limit or earlier loss/closure threshold is not. A 30-day cutoff was suggested, not approved.
4. **Viability threshold:** the CEO must recommend viability after analysis; the owner must approve every new venture, but no minimum projected return, payback period, or evidence-confidence threshold has been set.

## Authority and interpretation boundary

This is an operating-policy draft, not permission to change the current runtime or conduct external activity. All nine live authorities remain denied. Model-generated proposals, viability analyses, and task content are untrusted data: they cannot grant authority, change budgets, or authorize actions. The existing synthetic orchestration plan and historical finance records retain their stated scope and status; this draft does not accept a phase gate or rewrite historical decisions.
