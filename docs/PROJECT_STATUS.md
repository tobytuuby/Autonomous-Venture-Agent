## Gate 2 decision

Gate 2 was explicitly classified **CLOSED** by the project operator on
2026-09-18 after review of the full provider rerun, the committed report and
trace, and provider-side Completions log evidence. The provider evidence remains
classified as **externally configured but independently unverifiable**. This
decision records human acceptance of the evidence for Gate 2; it does not claim
a cryptographic or platform-signed attestation.

## P2 boundary and constraints

P2 may implement only opportunity, evidence, thesis, scoring, review, cooldown,
expiration, quarantine, and replay contracts. It must preserve the P0/P1
authority model and may not grant CEO execution authority, spawn Venture
Managers or workers, launch ventures, request capital, execute payments, or
perform any other external action.

Stage 1 and all real capital actions remain blocked. P7 remains the first phase
where real external action may be considered.
