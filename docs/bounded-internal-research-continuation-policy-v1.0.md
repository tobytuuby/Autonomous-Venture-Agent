# Bounded internal research continuation policy v1.0

Date: 2026-11-01. Authorization source: owner's Task02 implementation instruction.
Scope: existing Owner-approved INTERNAL_RESEARCH_ONLY proposal and existing signed
PREPARATION_ONLY hierarchy. This reconciles only conflicting internal-research
wording in AGENTS.md and owner-venture-approval-policy-v1.0.md. Historical policies
remain immutable. It does not enable pre-approval delegation or production deployment.

The original human approval binds the complete proposal digest, approved research
scope and workflow handoff. It authorizes up to three sequential research rounds
without renewed Owner review. A changed proposal, external operating scope, budget
or authority requires a separate approval. Workflow IDs never reset a proposal's
allowance. The full authoritative proposal digest is the accounting identity.

R1 is the existing research round, including legacy reservations. R2 and R3 require
a fresh signed CEO NEEDS_MORE_RESEARCH assessment bound to the exact prior snapshot,
a distinct objective, an explicit unanswered question, and prior signed evidence.
The signed journal persists round identity, venture/workflow/proposal bindings and
prior evidence. Manager corrections retain their existing version/effort rules;
corrections neither increment research rounds nor replenish inference allowance.

Worker reservations, including failed/ambiguous calls, consume at most four calls
per round and twelve total per approved proposal across all workflow IDs. Exact
duplicates consume no new allowance and grant no new execution. Replays, changed
requests, unauthenticated authority, skipped rounds and R4 fail closed. Existing
central ProviderQuota remains 94 calls / 16384 output tokens daily. Reservation
must commit before paid provider access; no crash refunds or automatic paid retry.

CEO directs Manager; Manager proposes assignments; CEO signs the decomposition;
Workers return evidence through Manager. At R3 the signed CEO recommendation must
be PROCEED_TO_MVP or REJECT_OR_DEFER. PROCEED_TO_MVP is a recommendation for a
separately authorized internal development scope, never launch or capital approval.
No fourth research round, payment, customer contact or deployment follows it.

Owner ideas API and existing CTKFamily page show observed authoritative round,
allowance, objectives, signed recommendation, artifacts, exhaustion/disposition and
owner action. Missing/stale observations are unavailable. Dollar spend and dollar
authority are null/unavailable unless an authoritative financial ledger supplies
them; call/token reservations are not money. Research approval offers no repeat
review action. Launch and capital boundaries remain denied.

Evidence classes remain separate: implemented, isolated tests, pushed, deployed,
runtime verified and independently verified. Production acceptance requires a
genuine signed second-round CEO → Manager → Workers execution; synthetic fixtures
do not satisfy that requirement. ADV-001–ADV-049 and /49 are unchanged.
