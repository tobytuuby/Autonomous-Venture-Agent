## Assessment and remaining limitations

The implemented local handoff is reviewable and fail-closed in both requested
modes. Operators can deliver independently signed preparation inputs without
giving any signing key to the service or model. Missing authority stays visibly
pending rather than being represented as execution. Signed dispatch remains
bounded preparation only, with synthetic worker data and no operational venture
admission, funds, launch, customer contact or deployment.

No real signer, CEO decision, advisory business assessment, owner discussion or
owner approval is configured or claimed. A trusted signer must externally sign
the exact CEO receipt and assignment command before real preparation delivery
can occur. Signatures establish signer attribution and content binding; they
cannot prove that a person/model actually reasoned or that provider execution
occurred. Provider execution is not performed or independently verified here.

The systemd timer is transient and disappears on reboot. Durable /etc installation
was outside this project-contained change. Privileged rollback, malicious root
or replacement of trusted source code require independently managed deployment
and external audit anchoring; local hash chains cannot defeat those threats.
Outstanding/expired invalid request files require operator inspection and must
not be automatically re-signed or renewed. No production manager/worker runtime
has been attached.

human review. Independent acceptance is pending. Prior phases and Gate 2 status
remain unchanged. No real CEO, owner signing key, capital action, spend, launch
