Studio Restock Kit

Weekly supplies worksheet · v1.0 · One row per item and unit. Work in base units (e.g. kg); pack size uses that same unit.

Enter physical usable stock, confirmed inbound stock, your reorder point, target, supplier pack size and pack price. A row at or below its reorder point orders up to target, rounded to whole packs. Inbound counts toward stock position. Numbers are planning suggestions; inspect every order. Prices below are fictional USD examples.

Changes stay in this tab until downloaded. Save inputs before closing. Import replaces all rows. No automatic orders or network connections.

Estimate excludes delivery, tax, discounts and supplier minimum orders. Do not add kg, litres and pieces together. The total adds money only. Read START-HERE.md for setup, checks and limitations.
