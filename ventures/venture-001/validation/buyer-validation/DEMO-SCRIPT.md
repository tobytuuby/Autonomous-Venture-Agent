# 5-minute private product demonstration

1. Download and extract `ventures/venture-001/Studio-Restock-Kit-v1.0.zip` from the repository. Open restock.html locally. No account or internet is required.
2. Click **Load fictional example**. Review stoneware clay: on hand 12 kg, reorder at 15, target 40, supplier pack 12.5 kg at 22 per pack. The tool suggests 3 packs (37.5 kg), estimate 66.
3. Review clear glaze: inbound stock is counted; if stock position exceeds the reorder point, there is no reorder.
4. Edit quantity to mimic a real studio situation (do not enter private or confidential business data). Observe the estimate update.
5. Download inputs CSV, then results CSV. Explain that the former preserves edits and the latter is a purchasing review, NOT an automatically transmitted order.
6. Ask participant to add a row unassisted. Note hesitation, confusion and errors. Ask whether their existing free method already does the job.
7. Show price concept only after use; capture their purchase answer without pressure.

**Unverified:** Browser-level controls, download/print and accessibility should be tested; calculation-only test coverage does not prove browser usability. Never claim this demonstration was run with actual prospects.
