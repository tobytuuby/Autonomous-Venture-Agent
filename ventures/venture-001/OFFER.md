# Venture #1 — Studio Restock Kit

**Product:** Offline spreadsheet-like restock worksheet for independent pottery studios.
**Suggested test price:** $12 one-time digital download, subject to demand testing.
**Customer:** Owner/operator of a small ceramics studio tracking clay, glazes, packaging and consumables without paid inventory software.

## Value proposition
A small studio should be able to make its weekly supply check in one sitting: enter stock, inbound deliveries and reorder targets, then see proposed pack quantities and a simple purchase-review list. No account, recurring subscription, cloud storage or supplier integration.

## Proposed listing copy (UNPUBLISHED)
**Title:** Studio Restock Kit — an offline weekly reorder worksheet for pottery studios

**Short description:** Count clay, glazes and studio consumables; flag items below your reorder point; estimate pack quantities and a purchasing budget. A downloadable, browser-based worksheet that works offline.

**What's included:** `restock.html`, `START-HERE.md`, editable example data through the worksheet, CSV import/export and print view. Buyers set their own supplier prices, pack sizes and reorder thresholds.

**Clear disclaimers:** No automatic orders or forecasting; estimates exclude tax, shipping and discounts. Not a replacement for inventory management software.

**Call to action:** Download the kit and try your next weekly restock check in one sitting.
