# Venture #1 operations — conditional plan, NOT launch authorization

## Preparation
- Confirm that opening restock.html works offline and that sample counts, validation, import/export and print all work.
- Package product files in a ZIP with instructions. Verify extraction and integrity.
- Decide license terms, buyer support email, refund policy and privacy wording before accepting payments.
- Use existing selected PayPal Business integration only when separately authorized; do not create a second financial-provider account.
- Verify fees and merchant eligibility against current PayPal terms. No fee assumptions are treated as facts here.

## Customer validation, not yet conducted
Interview or survey 5–10 independent studio operators through a separately authorized outreach channel. Ask about their *last* supply stockout, their current workaround, weekly time spent, and whether a $12 one-time offline kit would be worth purchasing. Avoid leading questions. Offer a free demonstration and record explicit permission before follow-up.

A modest launch-readiness rule: at least 3 participants describe recurring stockout or ordering pain, at least 2 test the tool successfully, and at least 1 gives a credible purchase intent or pays through an approved channel. These thresholds are provisional, not statistically significant proof of demand.

## After explicit authorization for live operation
1. Publish listing using approved merchant and product-delivery channel; test checkout and digital fulfillment.
2. Track visits, demos, purchases, refunds, fees, support time and actual net profit.
3. Improve product only from observed buyer feedback. Avoid paid ads until conversion is known.
4. Stop if real contribution margin is nonpositive or there is no evidence of willingness to pay after a bounded validation cycle.

## Owner and boundaries
The CEO can propose, the manager can organize, and workers can draft offline assets. No agent is authorized to publish, buy ads, contact customers, issue refunds or move funds. Human approval is still required for any new live authority.
